Circular Waste Diversion

What Happens to Waste Food? — Pune Municipal Valorization Hierarchy

When food is spoiled, expired, or deemed unconsumable during FSSAI thermal inspection, it is never landfilled. It enters a strict 4-stage municipal bio-conversion pathway.

View Live Conversion Runs
1

Triage & Moisture Sorting

Incoming spoiled batches are scanned, depackaged, and segregated into high-moisture slurries (suitable for methanation) vs fibrous organics (suitable for composting).

📍 All 4 Municipal Hubs
2

Aerobic Composting

Fibrous food scraps undergo controlled 21-day thermophilic aerobic degradation at 55–65°C. Generates Grade-A microbial organic compost for local urban farms.

📍 Hadapsar Organic Composting Hub (Yield: 35%)
3

Biogas / Bio-CNG

Liquid food sludges and gravies enter anaerobic biomethanization digesters. Methane is scrubbed to 95% purity and bottled as Bio-CNG for municipal waste trucks.

📍 Kothrud Biogas Digestion Plant (Yield: 10 m³/100kg)
4

Pelleting & Material Recovery

Cardboard, liners, and clean packaging are baled for paper mills. Residual solid digestate is dried and pressed into high-calorific biomass fuel briquettes.

📍 Shivajinagar Material Recycling Hub
