What Happens to Waste Food? — Circular Valorization Protocol

Food deemed inedible or failed safety verification is routed into biological and material transformation pipelines across PMC hubs:

View Active Conversion Runs
1 Aerobic Composting
Solid kitchen scraps and spoiled produce undergo 21-day microbial aeration at 55–65°C.
🌾 100 kg Waste → 35 kg Organic Compost
2 Anaerobic Biogas / Bio-CNG
High-moisture cooked food sludges ferment in sealed anaerobic digesters producing methane.
⚡ 100 kg Waste → 8–12 m³ Bio-CNG Fuel
3 Material Recycling
Inorganic food packaging, tin containers, cartons, and plastic liners are segregated and baled.
📦 95% Secondary Raw Material Recovery
4 Biomass Briquettes
Dry stalks, vegetable fibers, and fibrous digestate are compacted into high-density fuel briquettes.
🔥 Coal-Replacement Clean Boiler Fuel
