Diamond Nodes:

Represents relational associations: MAKES, SCHEDULED FOR, HANDLED BY, DONATED BY, DELIVERS TO, RECORDED AT, PROCESSED IN, GENERATES, REVIEWS.

ATTR
Oval Nodes:

Entity attributes. Ovals marked with (PK) are primary identifiers; ovals marked with (FK) denote foreign keys referencing parent tables.

1 N

1 : N (One-to-Many), N : 1 (Many-to-One), and 1 : 1 structural constraints enforcing relational integrity without data redundancy.

Part 4: DBMS Examination Defense (Third Normal Form - 3NF Proof)

First Normal Form (1NF)

Every table in the relational mapping contains strictly atomic (indivisible) scalar columns. There are zero multi-valued attributes or repeating groups (e.g. food portions and units are separated, contacts are distinct fields).

Second Normal Form (2NF)

Every table satisfies 1NF and contains single-attribute primary keys (User_ID, Donation_ID, Pickup_ID, Center_ID, etc.). Therefore, no non-prime attribute suffers from partial key functional dependency.

Third Normal Form (3NF)

Every table satisfies 2NF and contains zero transitive dependencies. Specialized organization details (License_No, NGO_Type, Website) are factored into NGO_RECEIVER rather than bloating USER.
